              About the AI

              Xenova/t5-small is a quantized T5 model run locally with Transformers.js 2.17.2 and WebAssembly. Loading it downloads model files from Hugging Face and runtime files from jsDelivr. Your assignment text is not sent to either service.

              Until the model finishes, or if it’s unavailable, the summary comes from a local rule-based parser. That same parser always powers the checklist, dates, rubric, risk flags, plan, and quiz. Compare all results with your original brief.

              The AI summary focuses on the assignment goal and hand-in instructions; use the dated and grading cards for those details.
